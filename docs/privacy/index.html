Tab Dormancy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026 · Applies to the Tab Dormancy extension for Microsoft Edge

Tab Dormancy is a browser extension that closes tabs after your device has been idle, based on rules you configure, and keeps a local history so you can restore them. This policy explains what data the extension accesses, how it is used, where it is stored, and the choices you have. The extension is not affiliated with Microsoft.

1. Data the extension accesses and stores

To provide its single purpose, the extension accesses the following data from your browser:

  • Tab metadata: the URL, title, and domain of open tabs, plus state such as active, pinned, audible, muted, discarded, grouped, and last-accessed time. The URLs and titles of open tabs may be considered web history. The extension does not use the browser's history API to read your stored browsing history.
  • Tab-group metadata: group title, color, and collapsed state.
  • Idle and lock state: whether the browser reports the device as active, idle, or locked.
  • Extension data: your settings and the cleanup strategies you create.
  • Session identifier: a randomly generated identifier stored in session storage and cleared when the browser session ends. It is used to group restored tabs correctly.

When the extension closes a tab, it creates a local history record. A record can contain the tab's URL, title, domain, close time, trigger, window ID, tab-group metadata, pinned state, tab index, strategy name, an optional browser session ID, and whether the tab has been restored.

The extension does not access page content, form data, passwords, cookies, keystrokes, mouse movements, downloads, bookmarks, or your location. It does not inject content scripts and does not read the text of the pages you visit.

2. How the data is used

The data is used only to:

  • Evaluate the cleanup rules you configure and show a preview of which tabs would be closed.
  • Close the tabs selected by your enabled strategy and record them in the local history.
  • Display, search, filter, restore, or delete history entries at your request.
  • Remember your settings and strategies.

3. Storage and sharing

All data is stored locally on your device using the browser's extension storage APIs (chrome.storage.local and chrome.storage.session). The extension does not use cloud sync, does not make network requests, and does not include analytics, telemetry, advertising, or crash reporting. It does not transmit data to the developer or to any third party. We do not sell, rent, or transfer user data.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Closed-tab history is kept for 30 days by default, with a maximum of 5,000 records. You can change the retention period in Settings.
  • You can delete individual history records or clear all history at any time from the History page.
  • Uninstalling the extension removes its local storage, subject to the browser's own storage cleanup behavior.
  • Settings and strategies remain until you change or delete them, or uninstall the extension.

5. Permissions

Permission Why it is needed
tabs Read tab URL, title, and state; close tabs selected by your rules; restore tabs from local history.
storage Save settings, strategies, and local closed-tab history on your device.
idle Detect when the device is idle or locked to trigger automatic cleanup.
alarms Schedule a one-shot delayed cleanup after the screen is locked. No recurring alarms or polling are used.
tabGroups Read tab-group metadata for rules that protect grouped tabs, and restore closed tabs into a group.
sessions Optionally restore a closed tab with its navigation stack when you explicitly request a full restore. Falls back to opening the URL if the session is unavailable.

6. Children

The extension is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top of this page will change, and the updated policy will be posted at this URL.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue at github.com/Aj002Th/tab-dormancy/issues. Please do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue.

Tab Dormancy

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 25 日 · 适用于 Microsoft Edge 版 Tab Dormancy 扩展

Tab Dormancy 是一个浏览器扩展：当设备空闲达到你设定的时间后，它按你配置的规则关闭标签页，并在本地保留关闭历史，方便你恢复。本政策说明扩展访问哪些数据、如何使用、存储在哪里，以及你有哪些选择。本扩展与 Microsoft 无隶属关系。

1. 扩展访问和存储的数据

为实现单一用途，扩展会访问以下浏览器数据：

  • 标签页元数据：打开标签页的 URL、标题、域名，以及是否当前标签、是否固定、是否发声、是否静音、是否已卸载、是否属于标签组、最后访问时间等状态。打开标签页的 URL 和标题可能被视为浏览历史。扩展不会调用浏览器的 history API 读取你已保存的浏览历史。
  • 标签组元数据：标签组名称、颜色和折叠状态。
  • 空闲和锁屏状态：浏览器报告的设备处于活跃、空闲还是锁屏状态。
  • 扩展数据：你的设置和你创建的清理策略。
  • 会话标识：一个随机生成的标识，保存在会话存储中，浏览器会话结束后清除，用于正确分组恢复的标签页。

当扩展关闭标签页时，会在本地创建一条历史记录。记录可能包含标签页的 URL、标题、域名、关闭时间、触发方式、窗口 ID、标签组元数据、固定状态、标签页序号、策略名称、可选的浏览器会话 ID，以及是否已恢复。

扩展不会访问页面正文、表单数据、密码、Cookie、键盘输入、鼠标移动、下载内容、书签或你的位置。扩展不注入内容脚本，也不读取你所访问页面的文字内容。

2. 数据如何使用

数据仅用于：

  • 评估你配置的清理规则，并预览哪些标签页将被关闭。
  • 关闭当前启用策略选中的标签页，并记录到本地历史。
  • 按你的请求展示、搜索、筛选、恢复或删除历史记录。
  • 记住你的设置和策略。

3. 存储与共享

所有数据都通过浏览器的扩展存储 API（chrome.storage.local 和 chrome.storage.session）保存在你的设备本地。扩展不使用云同步，不发起网络请求，不包含分析、遥测、广告或崩溃上报，也不会把数据发送给开发者或任何第三方。我们不会出售、出租或转让用户数据。

4. 保留与删除

  • 关闭历史默认保留 30 天，最多 5,000 条。你可以在设置中修改保留期限。
  • 你可以随时在历史页面删除单条记录或清空全部历史。
  • 卸载扩展会移除其本地存储，具体行为受浏览器自身存储清理机制影响。
  • 设置和策略会一直保留，直到你修改、删除它们或卸载扩展。

5. 权限说明

权限 用途
tabs 读取标签页 URL、标题和状态；关闭规则选中的标签页；从本地历史恢复标签页。
storage 在本地保存设置、策略和关闭历史。
idle 检测设备空闲或锁屏状态，触发自动清理。
alarms 在锁屏后安排一次延迟清理；不使用周期闹钟或轮询。
tabGroups 读取标签组元数据，用于保护标签组的规则，以及恢复标签组。
sessions 仅在你主动选择“完整恢复”时，用于恢复标签页的导航栈；会话不可用时回退为打开 URL。

6. 儿童隐私

本扩展不面向儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

7. 政策变更

我们可能更新本政策。页面顶部的生效日期会随之更新，更新后的政策仍发布在此 URL。

8. 联系方式

如有隐私相关问题，请在 github.com/Aj002Th/tab-dormancy/issues 提交 issue。请不要在公开 issue 中包含敏感个人信息。
